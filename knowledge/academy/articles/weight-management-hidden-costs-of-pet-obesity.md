> Leaflet explaining three hidden costs of pet obesity: reduced lifespan, poor health, and higher expenses

This document is designed to help you, as a veterinarian, communicate effectively the importance of addressing pet obesity with your clients. Obesity in pets is not just an aesthetic concern, it is a serious health issue that can lead to significant complications:

- reduced life span,
- reduced long-term health because of associated conditions such as metabolic disorders, joint issues or cardiovascular disease,
- increased veterinary costs.
By sharing this information, you can educate pet owners on the hidden risks associated with obesity and empower them to take proactive steps toward weight management, improving the health and happiness of their pets.
