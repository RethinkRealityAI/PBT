> Maintaining a healthy weight is a cornerstone of overall well-being and health in pets.

However, discussing weight management with pet owners can often be a sensitive and complex topic. This 4 step guide is designed to help you, as a veterinarian, with a structured approach to discussing overweight and obesity with pet owners:

1. Help owners understand their pet's body condition
2. Find common ground around begging and treating habits
3. Explain the benefits of avoiding the hidden costs of obesity [Link to Page - Weight Management: Hidden costs of pet obesity]
4. Recommend a comprehensive approach to healthy lifestyle and feeding
By following these 4 steps, you will be able to have a clear communication, provide actionable advice, and empower pet owners to make informed decisions about their pet's health.
