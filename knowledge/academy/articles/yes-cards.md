> Understanding the ingredients in pet food is essential for you, as veterinarians, so you can effectively recommend the diets and address pet owners' concerns.

This document offers an in-depth look at the key components used in pet food, allowing you and your clients to better understand the science behind these formulations and how these specific ingredients contribute to the health and well-being of cats and dogs:

- Corn, rich in amino acids that support skin and bladder health, and antioxidants that protect cells.
- Grains like wheat and rice, which provide energy, promote digestion, and supply essential nutrients.
- Dehydrated meats and by-products, protein-rich ingredients that are crucial for muscle growth and maintaining a healthy coat.
- Antioxidants, which are vital for preserving food freshness, preventing spoilage, and ensuring pets receive safe, nutrient-rich meals.
