> Helping a pet achieve a healthy weight is a significant accomplishment that deserves to be recognized.

Weight management requires time, consistency, and active participation from owners, often involving changes in feeding habits, exercise routines, and regular veterinary check-ins.
The Healthy Weight Award is a tool you can use to acknowledge and celebrate the dedication of pet owners who have successfully helped their pet lose excess weight. By presenting this award, you not only reinforce the importance of maintaining a healthy weight but also strengthen the bond between your team and the pet owner. It’s an opportunity to encourage continued commitment and to highlight the positive impact of their efforts on their pet’s health and longevity.
