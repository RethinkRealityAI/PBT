> This document ""10 most common pet owner preconceived ideas"" offers guidance to veterinarians on how to address common misconceptions about pet nutrition, particularly for puppies and kittens. It highlights the importance of effective communication with your clients, especially when addressing popular but sometimes misguided online advice.

1. “I Know What’s Right For My Pet” / “This Product Is Not Safe For My Pet”

2. “I Want To Feed My Pet A Raw / Barf Diets”

3. “I Want To Feed My Pet A Grain Free Diet”

4. “I Want To Feed My Pet A Whole Food/Cooked/Natural/Organic Diet”

5. “I Don’t Want To Use Manufactured Pet Food”

6. “I Want To Feed My Pet A Cheaper/More Affordable Diet”

7. “My Pet Does Not Need A Prescription Diet”

8. “I Don’t Want To Feed My Pet Dry And/Or Wet”

9. “My Puppy/Kitten Does Not Need Specific P/K Food”

10. “My Puppy/Kitten Is Ready To Switch To Adult Food”

11. “I Know Best How Much I Should Give My Pet”"
