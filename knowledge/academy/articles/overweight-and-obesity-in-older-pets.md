> In this video, dr. Tabitha Hookey (DVM, Dipl. ACVIM Nutrition) will take you through the most common questions regarding overweight and obesity in older pets.

> **Should we make senior overweight pets lose weight?**
>
> In senior pets, particularly in case of obesity, weight loss will improve mobility and quality of life.
>
> ## Could you share tips to help a senior pet lose weight?
>
> For senior pets with obesity, weight loss plans integrate general principles of therapeutic weight reduction with additional consideration of the implications of age associated comorbid health conditions. Consulting with a veterinary nutritionist may be recommended for complicated cases.
